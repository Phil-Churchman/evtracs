EV-TRACS

A suite of software tools for configuring, running, visualising and analysing outputs of simulations of EV trips and charging or battery swaps, using open source mapping and routing libraries. It covers cleaning and analysing captured vehicle tracking data, configuring simulation parameters and the geographic boundary, running the simulation, and viewing and analysing what it produced.

This site is a read-only view of published scenarios. The model overview walks through the steps behind them.

Work Package 4

The project runs as six work packages that work collaboratively. Work Package 4 covers its small-scale electric vehicle transport strand: modelling how electric two- and three-wheelers move around a town, how far they travel, and where and when they need to charge or swap batteries.

Those charging loads are what connect the transport strand to the rest of the project — they become demand a mini-grid has to meet. EV-TRACS is the modelling and visualisation toolset built for that work, at the Institute for Transport Studies, University of Leeds.

The Moving IMPACT project

Integrated Means to Power Agriculture, Clean Cooking and Transportation

“Linking PV generation, agricultural production, small-scale EV transportation and national grids will be vital for ensuring the economic viability of rural mini-grids.”

The project brings together seven academic institutions in the UK, Kenya, Rwanda and Ghana, led by Imperial College London.
